Parent & Guardian Guide

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

Everything you need to know about enrolling your child in AutobotsDev Academy.

Does my child need their own laptop?

For in-person workshops in Gaborone, computers are provided at the hub. For online sessions, a basic laptop or desktop computer with internet access is recommended.

How are students supervised?

All sessions are conducted by vetted Autobots software engineers in a safe, collaborative environment with strict child protection policies.

When are classes held?

Classes run on weekend afternoons (Saturdays 09:00 AM, 12:00 PM & 02:00 PM, 05:00 PM) so they do not conflict with school hours.

Will my child build real projects?

Yes! Every student completes a live portfolio project (e.g. personal website, mobile game, or interactive quiz) and receives an Autobots Completion Certificate.
