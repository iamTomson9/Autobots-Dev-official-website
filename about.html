About AutoBots Dev

AutoBots Dev is led by a multidisciplinary founding team combining business leadership, software engineering, product development, and innovation in Botswana.

Our Origin & Purpose

Founded in Gaborone, Botswana by a group of passionate youth software engineers, AutoBots Dev was established to prove that African software development companies can build world-class, enterprise-grade applications while solving high-impact local challenges.

From building flagship mobile applications like Property Lynk (live on the Google Play Store with 2.4K+ users) to developing custom e-commerce engines like EasyOrder and mentoring over 500+ young coders through the AutoBots Academy, we are committed to engineering practical digital solutions for businesses, organisations, and communities across Botswana.
